Phase 1 · Audit of the current interface

Patterns that read as generated or generic

  • Boxes inside boxes. Three bordered 12 px cards (question, map, answer), then cards inside them (status, three tiles, list items). Every level has the same border and radius, so nothing leads.
  • Wizard numbering. Circled 1, 2, 3, 4 before each heading. The controls are not a sequence; the numbers add noise.
  • Coloured left border on the status card and an orange dashed note box: two very common template devices.
  • Space clichés on the globe. Random starfield, blue atmosphere glow and dark limb shading compete with the fire data.
  • Typeface pairing. Space Grotesk + Inter is one of the most used pairings in generated interfaces; Space Grotesk's quirky shapes also hurt digit reading.
  • Uppercase tracked eyebrows ("THE ANSWER", "YOUR QUESTION", "WHOLE PLANET") on almost every block.
  • Text glyphs as icons (▲ ▼ ● ▶) render differently per system font.
  • Emoji favicon (🔥): the drawing comes from the visitor's system emoji font, not from us.

Inconsistencies

  • Radii: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 999 px in use.
  • Font sizes: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 20, 22 and 24 px, with no scale.
  • Three palettes at once: blue accent UI, orange fire ramp, and viridis on the confidence map, plus a blue-orange diverging ramp.
  • Tiles wrap badly at 1024 px: "349,142 adjusted detections. A normal November week has about 259,694." breaks into six lines in a 105 px column.
  • Mobile: the question panel takes the whole first screen; the answer comes after the map and the time bar.
  • Selected button colour (light blue fill, dark text) is the strongest thing on screen, stronger than the fire data.
  • Era labels truncate ("Old…") under the time slider.

Kept as is (works well)

  • The question → map → answer structure, the plain-language verdict, the calendar, the colour-blind-safe confidence ramp, keyboard focus outlines.

10 · Earth globe: the two requested prototypes

Working inside the real app, same data, rotation, zoom, timeline, selection and tooltips. Only the drawing of the base map differs. The switch at the top of the map changes it live; the default stays "Current".
Current

Flat dark land on navy, starfield, blue glow.

Familiar, but the space effects compete with the data.

Option 1 · Realistic (not chosen, removed)

Natural Earth II relief and land cover, soft light from the upper left, thin atmosphere, faint borders. The texture darkens as you zoom in so fires keep contrast.

Impressive at world scale. Where fire is dense (southern Africa in August) the fire layer and the tan land blend together. Costs more per frame (about 25 ms while spinning against about 5 ms).

Option 2 · Minimal scientific

Neutral graphite sphere, slightly lighter land, hairline borders and limb, faint graticule, no stars or glow. Fire squares drawn crisp, without the soft halo.

Highest contrast for fire and cheapest to draw. Less "wow" in a pitch; relies on the data to be the picture.

Open full screen: standard · minimal. The switch is now always on the map. Previews load the full app (about 10 MB once, then cached).

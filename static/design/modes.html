2 · Architecture

One page, one state. The mode only decides which controls are shown. Area, time, playback position and display settings live in the same place for both, so switching never resets them (they are already kept in the address, except the playback card).

Presentation (default). Globe fills the screen. Top bar: name, place search, mode switch. A small metrics card. Timeline full width at the bottom with play, speed, steps, date and year range.

Research, sidebar open. Left: research sidebar, sections A to E (controls). Right: analysis panel F (results, charts, export). Timeline under the globe. Both panels fold.

Research, collapsed. A thin rail of section letters stays; the globe takes the space. The analysis panel folds to a compact metrics card. On phones both panels become drawers from the bottom.
